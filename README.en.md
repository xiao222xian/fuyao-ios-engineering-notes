# Fuyao Zhiyuan | College Application & AI Career Planning Platform

[中文](README.md) · **English**

![Platform architecture / 平台架构](assets/platform-architecture.png)

**A platform that combines admissions data, recommendation algorithms and AI consultation with student applications, institutional workspaces and service operations.**

## What we build

Fuyao Zhiyuan supports college-application planning in China’s Gaokao admissions context, serving students, parents and educational consulting organizations. It connects institution and major discovery, score/rank analysis, personalized recommendations, application-plan management, AI consultation, plan diagnosis, subject-choice and interest exploration, career planning and report delivery.

Individual users can research and organize choices. Institutional users can manage student records, edit plans, continue consultation and planning cases, and access deliverables.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | What the platform provides | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Admissions information | Institutions, majors, historical results, enrollment plans, regional batches, score-distribution tables and policies | A consistent information context for comparison |
| Personalized recommendations | Score/rank matching, subject eligibility, preferences, filters and risk grouping | A manageable set of comparable candidates |
| Plan management and diagnosis | Group/major selection, editing, ordering, statistics, exports and existing-plan analysis | Connect discovery, composition and review |
| AI advisor | Natural-language consultation, business tools, data lookup, filter updates and explanations | Move from questions to structured actions |
| Subject and interest exploration | Subject guidance, career-interest/personality assessments and major/career information | Discuss interests alongside academic conditions |
| Career planning and reports | Initial profiles, multi-turn interviews, goals, planning records and document delivery | Preserve the outputs of a consultation process |
| Clients and institutional services | WeChat Mini Program, Web, iOS and Android engineering, institutional and operations modules | Support mobile use, desktop editing and service work |

## How the platform works

Data provides the information base. Algorithms organize eligible candidates and relative risk. AI interprets questions and calls business tools. Applications support user actions, while workspaces connect service records and deliverables.

![Recommendation pipeline / 推荐流程](assets/recommendation-pipeline.png)

Our implementation combines normalized admission context, rule-based candidate filtering, group-aware recommendations, structured agent tools, shared backend services and managed report delivery.
