# Product Capabilities | From Discovery to Plans and Delivery

[中文](PRODUCT_CAPABILITIES.md) · [English overview](../README.en.md)

## Admissions, institutions and majors

Queries are organized by institution, major, region, batch, year and subjects. Institution profiles link to enrollment plans, historical scores/ranks and policies; major records connect classifications, descriptions, courses and career directions.

Name searches, categories and filters lead into details, candidate comparison and application selection. Entry points retain the relevant profile and query context.

## Student profiles and personalization

Profiles contain scores, ranks, region, batch and selected subjects. Manual rank inputs and estimated information have distinct sources. These conditions are reused by queries and recommendations.

Preferences include institution and major directions, locations, institution attributes and score/rank ranges. They can be entered through the interface or translated from AI consultation into structured conditions.

## Recommendations and plans

Results organize candidates by relative risk and support major groups and child majors. Users select candidates, change composition and order, inspect statistics, and save or export the plan.

Group summaries, child selections and manual order remain distinct, keeping recommendations connected to editable plans.

## AI consultation and business actions

The advisor interprets questions in the context of the profile and current task. Business tools support lookup, filter changes, plan viewing/editing and statistics.

Structured results and natural-language explanations let users continue from understanding information to changing conditions and organizing choices.

## Plan diagnosis

Existing plans can enter through parsing and content-recognition workflows. Analysis combines the plan with the student context to discuss composition, relative risk and matching conditions, with document-related outputs.

File inputs and results are organized around business records for continued discussion and retrieval.

## Subject and interest exploration

Subject guidance, career-interest/personality assessments and major/career information supplement discussions about development directions. Results and history can be revisited. They are not treated as the sole basis for choosing a major.

## Career planning

Cases link initial information, goals, consultation, status and report documents. Multi-turn interaction clarifies background and constraints, followed by document services, downloading and sharing.

Resources and templates cover different development pathways. Resource organization and currently available service scope are managed separately.

## Clients

The WeChat Mini Program, Web, iOS and Android engineering provide different entry points. Mobile use emphasizes discovery and interaction; desktop workflows support information review and plan editing; institutional work links clients and deliverables.

Each client follows its own release cadence, with capabilities delivered according to product positioning, quality gates and release plans.
