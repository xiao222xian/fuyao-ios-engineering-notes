# Business Scenarios | Students, Parents and Institutions

[中文](BUSINESS_SCENARIOS.md) · [Platform overview](../README.en.md)

## From academic results to candidates

A student creates a profile, reviews candidates in the appropriate regional, batch and subject context, and filters by institution, major and location preferences. Details connect historical information, enrollment conditions and development directions. Formal conditions still require checking against applicable policies.

## Organizing a plan together

Students and parents select groups or majors, review composition, edit order and export a plan. The AI advisor can explain information and use business tools to adjust conditions or inspect the result. The workflow moves beyond a static recommendation list.

## Reviewing an existing application plan

Parsing-related entry points turn an existing file into records for analysis with the student profile. The service organizes readable and retrievable diagnostic outputs. Supported input scope depends on file format and the actual entry point.

## Discussing a longer-term direction

Initial information and goals lead into multi-turn consultation. Interests, constraints and development directions are clarified, with assessments and major/career information as context. Planning records connect the conversation to document delivery. Resources for further study and employment support case organization rather than replace case-specific verification.

## Institutional services

Desktop workspaces manage clients and records, inspect or edit plans, and connect planning conversations, allowances and reports. Linking profiles, plans and documents helps advisers continue an existing service case.

## Operations

Users, roles, entitlements, orders, promotions and service configuration support both individual and institutional use. Interfaces, permissions and ownership define access; commercial scope follows the actual service agreement.

## Connected workflow

Profiles establish context; recommendations and plans organize choices; AI supports explanation and actions; diagnosis and planning extend the service; documents preserve outputs. Institutional and operations modules provide the management layer behind these workflows.
